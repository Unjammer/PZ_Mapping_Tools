How limits are described

Game-confirmed structures were checked against the bundled Build 42.20 reference. Tool-enforced limits are safety or authoring policies and are not presented as engine constants. Representative preview means useful authoring output without claiming pixel-identical game-save parity. Every deliberate omission is listed as out of scope in the relevant feature reference.

For source provenance and port history, see UPSTREAM-HISTORY.md and CHANGELOG-PZTOOLS.md.
