PZ Mapping Tools - corresponding source offer
==============================================

The complete corresponding source code for the PZ Mapping Tools executables
in this distribution, including the scripts and project files used to build
them, is available without charge at:

    https://github.com/Unjammer/PZ_Mapping_Tools

Use the source revision identified by the release tag or commit accompanying
this binary release.

The portable Windows build dynamically links against Qt 5.14.2. The matching
unmodified Qt source archive is available at:

    https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/5.14/5.14.2/single/

If either source download becomes unavailable, any recipient of this binary
distribution may request the corresponding source through:

    https://github.com/Unjammer/PZ_Mapping_Tools/issues

For a period of at least three years after this release, the maintainer will
provide that source on a customary durable medium for no more than the
reasonable physical cost of performing the distribution.

This offer supplements, and does not replace or restrict, the rights granted
by the applicable GPL and LGPL license texts included with this distribution.
