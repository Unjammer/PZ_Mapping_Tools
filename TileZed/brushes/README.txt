TileZed custom BMP brushes
==========================

Place PNG brush masks in this folder or in settings/brushes.
Subfolders are scanned automatically while TileZed is running.

- Recommended image size: 32 x 32 pixels.
- Maximum accepted size: 128 x 128 pixels.
- Dark, opaque pixels paint cells.
- Transparent or light pixels are ignored.
- The image is centered on the cursor.

Use BMP Tools > Options > Custom PNG brush masks to select, import,
reload, or open the writable user brush folder.
